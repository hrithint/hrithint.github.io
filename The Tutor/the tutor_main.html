Core Curriculum — Master each domain

Fundamentals of Naval Architecture

Ship Geometry & Terminology: Lines plans, offsets, Block/Prismatic/Midship coeffs.
Intact & Damage Stability: Metacentric height (GM), GZ curves, free surface effect.
Resistance & Propulsion: Froude's law, resistance types, propeller selection.
Structural Mechanics: Still water/wave bending moments, shear forces, primary/secondary stresses.

Practical Ship Design & Statutory Computations

Statutory Documents: Freeboard & tonnage computations per IMO regulations.
Hull Form & Fairing: Maxsurf / Rhino 3D workflows from IGS formats.
Weight & COG Estimation: Lightship weight methods for preliminary design.
Classification Rules: Apply DNV, ABS, IRS for scantling & structural design.

Advanced Hydrodynamics & CFD

CFD High-Speed Vessels: Domain setup, meshing, resistance analysis for fast crew boats.
ANSYS Fluent Marine: Multiphase flow (VOF) and free surface modeling.
Propeller-Hull Interaction: Wake fraction (t), thrust deduction (w), aft-ship optimization.
Verification & Validation: Grid Convergence Index (GCI) & towing tank validation.

Offshore Engineering & Operations

Environmental Loads & RAOs: Metocean, JONSWAP, Morison/Diffraction, RAOs for semis.
Mooring Analysis: Static/dynamic line tension using Bentley MOSES.
Self-Elevating Barges: Transit stability, leg penetration & elevated conditions.
Modular Offshore Structures: Heavy weather shelters & temporary deck modifications.
